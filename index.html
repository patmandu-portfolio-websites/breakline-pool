8-BALL RULES
  1. The table is open after the break. The first ball legally pocketed on a regular shot assigns that player solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15).
  2. Each shot, hit one of your own balls first (any non-8 ball while the table is open), then pocket a ball or drive a ball to a rail.
  3. Pocket one of your own balls to keep shooting; otherwise play passes.
  4. A foul is a scratch, hitting the wrong ball first, or no ball pocketed and no rail after contact. The opponent gets ball-in-hand.
  5. Once your group is cleared, shoot the 8. Pocket it legally to win.
  6. Pocketing the 8 early, or fouling while pocketing it, loses the rack. An 8 pocketed on the break is re-spotted. No pockets are called.
9-BALL RULES
  1. On every regular shot, hit the lowest-numbered ball first.
  2. Pocket any ball legally to keep shooting. Pocket the 9 legally to win, even on a combination.
  3. A foul is a scratch, hitting the wrong ball first, or failing to pocket a ball or drive any ball to a rail after contact.
  4. After a foul, the opponent gets ball-in-hand anywhere on the table.
  5. Three consecutive fouls by one player lose the rack.
  6. After a legal break, the shooter may call one push-out. No hit or rail is required; the next player may take the shot or pass it back. Pocketed balls stay down, but a 9 pocketed on a push-out is spotted.

10-BALL RULES
  1. Ten balls are racked in a triangle with the 1 at the front and the 10 in the middle of the third row.
  2. On every regular shot, hit the lowest-numbered ball first.
  3. Pocket any ball legally to keep shooting. Pocket the 10 legally to win, even on a combination.
  4. A foul is a scratch, hitting the wrong ball first, or failing to pocket a ball or drive any ball to a rail after contact. The opponent gets ball-in-hand.
  5. Three consecutive fouls by one player lose the rack.
  6. After a legal break, the shooter may call one push-out. A 10 pocketed on the break or on a foul is spotted. Pockets are not called in this version.
